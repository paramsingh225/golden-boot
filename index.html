ABOUT THE GOLDEN SHOE

The European Golden Shoe is awarded to the leading goalscorer in European domestic league football, using a weighted points system. Since 1996/97, goals scored in different leagues have been assigned different points according to league coefficients. This archive celebrates the greatest goalscorers in European football history.
